## What would make the page worth their money

1. **There is currently nowhere on the internet that is actually theirs.** The
   domain their own directory listing points to (`texaseliteair.com`) is dead,
   and their most active channel (Instagram) links out to a Maps pin instead of
   a site. A live one-pager would be the first destination in their entire
   online footprint that they control and that says something in their own
   words.
2. **Their real strength — a 5.0-star reputation built since 2009 — is
   scattered across directories that don't agree with each other.** A single
   page can state their service area, phone number, and what they actually do
   in one place, rather than leaving a prospective customer to piece it
   together from mirror sites that disagree on their own street address.
3. **The phone number is the one fact every source agrees on, so it should be
   the most prominent thing on the page.** Right now it is buried inside
   third-party listing pages (Yelp, Nextdoor, Birdeye) surrounded by ads and
   other contractors' listings, not presented as theirs.
